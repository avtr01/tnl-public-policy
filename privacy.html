TNL Store Manager Privacy Policy

TNL uses eBay data only to prepare and manage the owner's store. Inventory and administration stay on the owner's computer.

Seller access and refresh tokens are encrypted on that computer. This public connection service does not store seller tokens or expose inventory or administration.

Account-deletion notices are encrypted in transit and in the notification inbox, verified against eBay signatures, and processed on the owner's computer. Matching retained data is placed into a durable removal review covering records, evidence and backups. TNL does not claim a no-data-storage exemption.

The owner can disconnect eBay through the local application or revoke access in eBay's account settings.
